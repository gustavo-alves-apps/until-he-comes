Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Applies to the Until He Comes iOS app

Overview

Until He Comes is a native iOS app that displays a continuously changing, non-chronological view of time alongside a short Christian message about watching and being ready for Christ's return. In short: Until He Comes does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no backend, no accounts, and no network connection at all — everything it shows and saves stays on your device. The rest of this page explains that plainly, section by section.

Information We Do Not Collect

Until He Comes does not connect to the internet in normal use. It does not have user accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any third-party tracking software embedded in it. We — the developer — never receive any information about you or your device, because nothing is ever sent anywhere. The one exception is the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use links in the app's Settings: when you tap one, this page opens in Safari inside the app, which loads it from the web like any website.

Information Stored On Your Device

The app does store a small amount of information, but only locally, on your own device, using standard iOS storage:

  • Preferences — your Animation Intensity, Haptics, Language, Notifications, and Lock My Reflections settings, saved via iOS's standard UserDefaults.
  • Reflections — if you choose to write a private reflection or prayer note, it is saved as a local file in the app's own storage area on your device. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared, and only you can read it, from within the app.

None of this leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data with it. We have no way to see, back up, or recover it, because we never had a copy.

Photos & the Home Screen Widget

The background photos you see in the app and its widget are bundled inside the app itself — the app does not access your device's photo library, camera, or any of your personal photos.

Microphone & Speech Recognition

If you choose to speak a reflection instead of typing it, Until He Comes uses your device's microphone only while you are recording, and only after you have given permission. Your speech is turned into text entirely on your device, using Apple's on-device speech recognition — the audio is never saved, never sent to Apple or to us, and never leaves your device. Only the resulting text is kept, as part of your reflection, stored locally like any other. If your device can't transcribe your language on-device, the microphone option is simply not shown.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the app schedules occasional reminders on your device using iOS's local notifications. They are created and delivered entirely on your device — no push service, no server, and no information sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Face ID & Passcode

If you turn on Lock My Reflections, the app asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode before showing your reflections. The check is done entirely by iOS — the app never has access to your face, fingerprint, or passcode, and only receives whether it succeeded or not.

Scripture Text

Scripture quotations shown in the app are drawn from public-domain Bible translations, chosen to avoid licensing restrictions on text that ships inside the app. English uses the World English Bible (WEB); Portuguese uses the João Ferreira de Almeida translation (1911/1914 revision); Spanish uses the Reina-Valera translation (1909); German uses the Luther Bible (1545); French uses the Martin Bible (1744); Italian uses the Riveduta Bible (1927); Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) uses the Union Version (和合本); Russian uses the Synodal Translation (1876); Japanese uses the Meiji/Taishō Translation (文語訳); Korean uses the Korean Revised Version (개역한글); Dutch uses the Statenvertaling (1637); Swedish uses the 1917 Church Bible; Norwegian (Bokmål) uses the Bibelselskapet translation; Danish uses the 1871 Danish Bible; Hungarian uses the Károli Bible (1590); Czech uses the Bible Kralická (1613); Romanian uses the Cornilescu translation — also public domain. As Until He Comes adds support for additional languages, each will use its own public-domain translation, for the same reason.

Children's Privacy

Because Until He Comes does not collect any personal data from anyone, regardless of age, it does not knowingly collect information from children either.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for instance, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does involve a network connection — this page will be updated and the "Effective" date above will change accordingly.

Ez egy független alkalmazás, amely nem kapcsolódik, nincs jóváhagyva vagy készítve semmilyen konkrét egyház vagy felekezet nevében, és nem helyettesíti a lelkipásztori vezetést, a teológiai tanulmányokat vagy a közösségi istentiszteletet.

Elfogadható Használat

Vállalod, hogy nem használod az Alkalmazást olyan módon, amely sérti a vonatkozó jogot, mások jogait sérti, vagy megkísérli megzavarni, visszafejteni vagy kinyerni az Alkalmazás forráskódját vagy erőforrásait azon túl, ami a tervezett személyes használatodhoz szükséges.

Szavatosság Kizárása

Az Alkalmazás "jelen állapotában" és "elérhetőség szerint" kerül biztosításra, bármilyen kifejezett vagy hallgatólagos garancia nélkül. Nem garantáljuk, hogy az Alkalmazás megszakítás nélküli, hibamentes vagy mindig elérhető lesz.

Felelősség Korlátozása

A törvény által megengedett legteljesebb mértékben Gustavo Alves nem vállal felelősséget az Alkalmazás használatából vagy használatának lehetetlenségéből eredő közvetett, véletlen vagy következményes károkért.

Az Alkalmazás Vagy E Feltételek Változásai

Időről időre frissíthetjük az Alkalmazást vagy e Feltételeket. Az Alkalmazás további használata a változások hatálybalépése után azt jelenti, hogy elfogadod a frissített Feltételeket.

Megszüntetés

Bármikor abbahagyhatod az Alkalmazás használatát a törlésével. Bármikor felfüggeszthetjük vagy megszüntethetjük az Alkalmazást, egészben vagy részben.
